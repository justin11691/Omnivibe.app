        2015 Honda Civic Brake Pad Replacement

        Difficulty: Medium Est. Time: 1.5 Hours
        Curated Tutorial Video: "Honda Civic Front Brake Pads (2012-2015)"

        Step-by-Step Instructions

        1. Loosen the wheel lug nuts, jack up the vehicle, and secure it on jack stands.
        2. Remove the front wheels completely to expose the brake caliper assembly.
        3. Remove the caliper slide pins and carefully lift the caliper off the rotor.
        4. Remove the old brake pads and install the new ones with grease at contact points.

        Required Tools

        • Floor Jack & Jack Stands
        • 12mm Wrench & Socket
        • C-Clamp

        Parts Needed

        • Front Brake Pads (Ceramic)
        • Brake Lubricant
