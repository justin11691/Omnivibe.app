What is an Error Code?

Error codes are diagnostic messages generated by vehicles, appliances, and electronics when something goes wrong. OBD-II codes (like P0420, P0171, P0300) are standardized codes emitted by your vehicle's onboard computer — they tell you exactly which system needs attention. Appliance error codes (like Whirlpool uL, LG OE, Samsung 5C) are manufacturer-specific alerts displayed on digital screens when a washing machine, dishwasher, dryer, or refrigerator detects a fault.

Omnivibe Guide's error decoder uses AI to instantly translate these cryptic codes into plain-language explanations, identify the most likely cause, and generate a complete step-by-step repair guide with the exact tools and parts you need.
