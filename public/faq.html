Why are the Web Studio and Local Companion Bridge separated?

Standard web browsers intentionally block raw UDP multicast and DTLS 1.2 sockets for security. Rather than faking smart-light sync or falling back to slow cloud APIs, OmniVibe Studio keeps omnivibe.app as a zero-install 60fps shader & soundscape studio, while our lightweight Local Companion Bridge / Desktop App runs natively on your LAN to drive Philips Hue (UDP 2100 DTLS), Govee (UDP 4001–4003), and WiZ (UDP 38899) at sub-50 ms latency.

How does music capture work on Windows/macOS vs. iOS/Android?

Windows & macOS: Use Desktop System Loopback (captures Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or games directly) or any Wired Line-In / USB audio interface.
iOS & Android: Mobile OS DRM policies (Android ALLOW_CAPTURE_BY_SYSTEM and iOS ReplayKit limits) frequently block third-party capture of streaming apps. On mobile, use OmniVibe Studio's Integrated Local Audio Player (zero DRM interference) or our Noise-Gated Acoustic Room Mic with adjustable noise gate and 0.5x–3.0x sensitivity.

Why do Bluetooth speakers or TVs feel slightly behind the lights?

Bluetooth codecs and TV soundbars introduce 60–180 ms of audio delay. Use OmniVibe Studio's Audio-to-Light Latency Offset Slider (0–250 ms) with saved profiles for Wired Speakers (0 ms), TV / Soundbar (85 ms), and Bluetooth Speakers (140 ms) so lights hit on the exact audible beat.
