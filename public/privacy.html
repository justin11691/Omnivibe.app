Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Applies to omnivibe.app, OmniVibe Studio Desktop & Local Companion Bridge

100% Local-First Architecture: OmniVibe Studio processes audio FFT telemetry, procedural soundscapes, 3D WebGL shaders, and smart-lighting frames strictly on your own device and local home network. We never record, store, or transmit microphone audio, line-in audio, loopback streams, or smart-light traffic to any cloud server.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your custom vibe presets, Studio skin selection, room zones, DSP calibration profiles (latency offset, master brightness, minimum-black floor), Local Companion Bridge settings, and Pro license key are saved locally in your device's storage so OmniVibe Studio runs completely offline. You can export or permanently erase this data anytime via Studio Settings → Reset to Factory Defaults.

2. Audio Capture & Microphone Permissions

Whether you use Desktop System Loopback / Wired Line-In (Windows & macOS), the Integrated Local Audio File Player, or the Noise-Gated Acoustic Room Microphone (iOS & Android), audio is analyzed exclusively in ephemeral local memory to compute real-time Bass, Mids, and Treble FFT bands. Audio is never recorded to disk or sent over the internet.

3. Local Companion Bridge & Smart-Home LAN Sync

Real-time lighting control for Philips Hue Entertainment API v2 (DTLS 1.2 PSK on UDP 2100), Govee LAN (UDP 4001–4003 + Razer 0xB1), and WiZ (raw JSON on UDP 38899) runs exclusively over your private local Wi-Fi network via the Local Companion Bridge (ws://127.0.0.1:47890). OmniVibe Studio never routes live lighting commands through cloud relays or third-party cloud HTTP APIs. Bridge credentials (such as Hue username and clientkey) and static bulb IPs remain strictly on your device.

4. Payments & Offline Pro Licensing

Lifetime Pro purchases ($19 one-time) are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store credit card numbers. Upon successful checkout, Cloudflare Workers/D1 store your generated offline license key and customer email solely for receipt delivery and cross-device key verification.

5. Contact

Questions regarding privacy or license recovery may be directed to support@omnivibe.app.
